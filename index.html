Build templates

Print these at full size and trace them onto the board before cutting. The template folder also includes image exports and earlier versions.

Elevation axis template

The telescope base carries a protractor so the observing angle is easy to set. Tape the template to the board to be cut, then drill through the marked locations.

Elevation mount template

The cone-horn design uses a wooden elevation mount of four parts. Front and back are cut from a 10-inch wide, 8-inch tall board 0.75 inches thick; the sides are two 14-inch balusters.
