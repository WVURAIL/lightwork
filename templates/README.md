# Build templates

- [Elevation axis](elevation-axis/elevation-axis.pdf): printable protractor, with [PNG](elevation-axis/elevation-axis.png) and [SVG](elevation-axis/elevation-axis.svg) exports.
- [Elevation mount, revision 2](elevation-mount/elevation-mount-r2.pdf): printable mount outline, with a [PNG](elevation-mount/elevation-mount-r2.png) export.
- [Earlier elevation mount](elevation-mount/history/): the original PDF and PNG.

Related exports stay together because they are different printable forms of the same template.
Their contents and dimensions are unchanged. Print at full size, without fitting to the page.
Editable originals are in [sources/templates](../sources/templates/).
